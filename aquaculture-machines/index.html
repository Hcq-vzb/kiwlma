Aquaculture Machines: Filling, Feeding & Water Treatment Lines

Feed packaging, drum filtration and hatchery equipment by KIWL Machine

KIWL Machine supplies aquaculture machines for fish and shrimp farms, hatcheries and feed mills: automatic feed pellet packaging lines, rotary drum filters, aeration systems and RAS water-treatment modules. Equipment is engineered for continuous duty in humid, saline environments and ships CE certified with installation support.

Feed production and packaging lines

Feed mills pair our pelletizing support equipment with automatic weighing, bagging and sewing lines for 25–50 kg bags, including clip-bagging options for shrimp feed that must stay dry and portioned.

Water treatment for RAS and ponds

Rotary drum filters remove suspended solids from recirculating aquaculture systems, while aeration blowers and diffusers hold dissolved oxygen at target levels. Protein skimmers and UV sterilizers complete the treatment train for hatcheries.

Hatchery and harvest support

We supply grading tables, harvest pumps and counting systems for fingerling and harvest operations, plus brine-shrimp and larval feed handling equipment for hatchery nurseries.
