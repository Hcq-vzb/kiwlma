Filling Lines for Africa

Market guides by KIWL Machine · Updated 2026-10-22

KIWL Machine supplies bottled water, juice, dairy and sauce filling lines to producers across Africa. Choose a market guide:

Nigeria

Nigeria is Africa's largest bottled water market, with demand in Lagos, Abuja, Kano and Port Harcourt outpacing local production.

Kenya

Kenya's dairy, juice and bottled water sectors are scaling fast, and Nairobi functions as the distribution hub for East Africa — equipment and spares flow to Uganda, Tanzania, Rwanda and Ethiopia.

Egypt

Egypt is the industrial heart of North Africa, with Morocco forming the region's other major equipment market.

Ghana

Ghana's cocoa, fruit and beverage processing sectors are driving demand for juice and water lines in Accra and Kumasi.
