Bottled Water Filling Lines in Nigeria

Market guide by KIWL Machine · Updated 2026-10-22

Nigeria is Africa's largest bottled water market, with demand in Lagos, Abuja, Kano and Port Harcourt outpacing local production. KIWL Machine supplies turnkey filling lines to Nigerian producers — from compact 2,000 BPH startup lines to 12,000 BPH integrated plants with washing, filling, capping and labeling.

Why Nigeria producers choose KIWL

Nigerian buyers choose KIWL for factory-direct pricing on CE-certified lines, 220-240V/50Hz electrical specs matched to the national grid, documented compatibility with diesel generator backup, and commissioning support for first-time plant owners.

Line configurations for Nigeria

A 3,000-6,000 BPH water line serves a city of 500,000-2 million people; sachet-to-PET upgraders typically start with a 5-gallon or 2,000 BPH line and scale to monobloc fill-rinse-cap systems. Juice and dairy lines add pasteurization and hot-fill modules.

Power and water adaptation

The Nigerian grid runs 220-240V / 50Hz with intermittent supply: lines ship with voltage protection and are specified to run on diesel generator backup. Water treatment (sediment, carbon, RO, UV) is sized from a source-water test — borehole or municipal.

Certification and compliance in Nigeria

CE certified with a full documentation pack (material traceability, sterilization validation, electrical certificates). For Nigeria we support NAFDAC registration and SON standards with equipment data; a local consultant is recommended for the 3-6 month registration timeline. Food-processing machinery often qualifies for import duty exemption of 10-25% via a customs broker.

Service, spare parts and support

On-site or remote commissioning, operator training, and a recommended 12-month kit of critical spares (seals, nozzles, valves) that can be hard to source locally. English support on WhatsApp with 48-hour response.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can your lines run on Nigerian grid power?

Yes — 220-240V/50Hz is standard, with voltage protection and diesel generator compatibility for unstable supply.

We provide CE certificates, material and sterilization documentation; registration itself goes through a local consultant.

What capacity suits a new water plant?

3,000-6,000 BPH covers a city of 500,000-2 million; smaller 2,000 BPH lines suit startup volumes.
