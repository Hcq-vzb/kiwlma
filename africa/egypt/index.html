Filling Lines in Egypt

Market guide by KIWL Machine · Updated 2026-10-22

Egypt is the industrial heart of North Africa, with Morocco forming the region's other major equipment market. KIWL Machine supplies filling lines to Egyptian beverage, juice and sauce producers, with Arabic-speaking technical support and export documentation prepared for L/C transactions.

Why Egypt producers choose KIWL

Egyptian buyers choose KIWL for CE-certified machinery at factory-direct prices, 220V/50Hz specs, Arabic-language support, and documentation packs prepared for letter-of-credit shipments.

Line configurations for Egypt

Beverage and juice lines from 2,000 to 12,000 BPH with rinsing, filling, capping and labeling; sauce and condiment lines with heated hoppers and piston fillers for viscous products.

Power and water adaptation

Egypt's grid runs 220V / 50Hz. Lines ship with standard industrial electrical protection; generator compatibility available. Water treatment matched to municipal or borehole sources.

Certification and compliance in Egypt

CE certified with full export documentation suitable for L/C; equipment and material certificates provided for import registration. Arabic documentation support available.

Service, spare parts and support

Remote commissioning with optional on-site visits, operator training (English/Arabic), critical-spares kit, and WhatsApp support.
