5 Gallon Decapping Machine

Published 2026-11-19 · KIWL Machine

The figures in this guide are the ones a supplier should be held to in writing. For a 5 gallon decapping machine in beverage filling plants production, they cover rate, tolerance, materials, control, changeover and acceptance.

What This Equipment Does

The test of a 5 gallon decapping machine is not the demonstration but the third month. At 1470–2120 units per hour for 1.5 L product, this configuration is designed to hold its rate once the novelty of commissioning has worn off.

Key Technical Specifications

The three figures that move the price most are accuracy, contact-part grade and the control platform. On this 5 gallon decapping machine the tolerance is 2.0 ml, the contact parts are SUS304 with sanitary fittings, and the controls run on a 10-inch colour touch.

Integration With Your Line

A 5 gallon decapping machine becomes part of the beverage filling plants production line when the same recipe reaches it, the same cleaning reaches it and the same fault code reaches the operator. This build is specified around those three handovers.

Capacity Planning and ROI

The ROI of a 5 gallon decapping machine comes from removing the manual lid-placement step at 1470–2120 cans per minute and from holding the torque window, which cuts rejects on the beverage filling plants production line.

Maintenance and Service

The 5 gallon decapping machine keeps its wear items accessible and its parameters exportable, so a shift leader can restore a setting without calling a technician and without losing 1470 units per hour of output.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the 5 gallon decapping machine integrate with an existing line?

It accepts a line encoder signal and can be indexed directly against the upstream conveyor, so it runs under the line PLC rather than as an isolated unit.

Does the 5 gallon decapping machine need a dedicated operator?

No. Once the format recipes are stored on the 10-inch colour touch, the changeover is a guided sequence, and the 8-minute routine replaces manual adjustment.

What is the connected load of the 5 gallon decapping machine?

The reference build draws 20–27 kW with a servo rotary head. Confirm starting current and supply arrangement before ordering.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.
