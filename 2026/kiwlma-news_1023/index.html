Bottled Water Filling Line

Published 2026-10-23 · KIWL Machine

A bottled water filling line sits on the critical path of the beverage filling plants production line, which makes the selection decision more consequential than the specification sheet suggests. What follows is the working detail — capacity, materials, control architecture and the acceptance checks — for teams evaluating a bottled water filling line.

What This Equipment Does

This configuration is built around 330 ml containers, which removes the format-guessing that usually slows beverage filling plants commissioning. Changeover then means swapping the guide set, not re-qualifying the whole line.

Key Technical Specifications

The specification that limits output on a bottled water filling line is usually changeover. At 12 minutes per 330 ml format and 1831–2481 units per hour, campaigns have to be planned around the stop.

Integration With Your Line

Buffer sizing upstream of the bottled water filling line determines whether the line absorbs a changeover. With 1831–2481 bottles per hour and a 12-minute format change, the buffer has to cover the stop, not the run.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Model the bottled water filling line on total cost over the equipment life, not on purchase price. A unit with 304/316L stainless steel contact parts and a 1.0 ml tolerance costs more to buy and less to run than a cheaper unit on the same beverage filling plants production line.

Maintenance and Service

Preventive discipline on a bottled water filling line is easier when the intervals are stated in cycles rather than months. At 1831–2481 units per hour, this build's seals and sensors reach end of life far sooner than a quarterly check would suggest.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can the bottled water filling line run plastic and glass at the same time?

This configuration handles 330 ml product on the specified format. Mixing materials usually means a cleaning and verification step between campaigns.

What is the actual connected load of the bottled water filling line?

The reference build draws 8.5–11.5 kW. Confirm the starting current and the supply arrangement at the site before ordering.

Does the bottled water filling line need a specific foundation?

No special foundation is required beyond a level floor with the clearance shown on the 3200 × 1900 × 2800 mm GA drawing. Confirm the floor loading for heavy configurations.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
