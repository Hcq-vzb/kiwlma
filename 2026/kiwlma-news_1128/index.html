Hot Juice Filling Monoblock

Published 2026-11-28 · KIWL Machine

A hot juice filling monoblock sits on the critical path of the beverage filling plants production line, which makes the selection decision more consequential than the specification sheet suggests. What follows is the working detail — capacity, contact materials, control architecture and the acceptance checks — for teams evaluating a hot juice filling monoblock.

What This Equipment Does

The practical test of a hot juice filling monoblock is not its peak rate but its behaviour at 8586 bottles per hour on a 5 L format, where product density shifts and viscosity climbs.

Key Technical Specifications

Ask for the electrical single-line diagram and the duty cycle, not just the power total. This hot juice filling monoblock draws 3–4 kW across a 3200 × 1900 × 2800 mm footprint with 316L stainless steel contact parts.

Integration With Your Line

Buffer upstream is the cheapest insurance on the beverage filling plants production line. A hot juice filling monoblock that can hold 8586–12086 units per hour but has nothing to draw from simply transfers the stoppage downstream, and a 8-minute changeover then becomes the beverage filling plants production line event.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Compare the hot juice filling monoblock against the cost of the manual step it removes, including the labour during changeover. At 8 minutes per format change and 8586 bottles per hour, the saving is measurable in shift hours.

Maintenance and Service

Train the people who run the shift, not only the commissioning engineer. On this hot juice filling monoblock the wear items are simple to identify — seals, drive, guide rails — and most beverage filling plants downtime is caused by a part that was available but not swapped.

Frequently Asked Questions

What spare parts should be held for the hot juice filling monoblock?

Seals, drive belt and guide rails are the wear items. For a line running 8586 bottles per hour, holding these on site is what protects availability.

Does the hot juice filling monoblock need a specific foundation?

No special foundation is required beyond a level floor with the clearance shown on the 3200 × 1900 × 2800 mm GA drawing. Confirm the floor loading for heavy configurations.

How long does a format changeover take on this hot juice filling monoblock?

With a standardised guide set the changeover is around 8 minutes. The first change on a new 5 L format takes longer because the guide set has to be set and taught.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
