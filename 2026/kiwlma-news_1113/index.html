Glass Bottle Washing Filling Machine

Published 2026-11-13 · KIWL Machine

Specifying a glass bottle washing filling machine for beverage filling plants is an exercise in matching three things: the rate you need, the product you run and the conditions you run it in. This guide covers each of the three, and ends with the checks to complete before you sign off.

What This Equipment Does

Consider what happens after the glass bottle washing filling machine: the container leaves at 8446–11446 bottles per hour with a deviation of ±3.0 ml, and every downstream step inherits that number. A tight tolerance is what keeps capping, labelling and inspection settings valid for a full run.

Key Technical Specifications

Do not compare a glass bottle washing filling machine on rated capacity alone. A 8446–11446 units per hour machine with a 3.0 ml tolerance and 304/316L stainless steel contact parts will out-produce a faster machine with looser accuracy over a full shift.

Integration With Your Line

Plan the buffer upstream and the transfer downstream so the glass bottle washing filling machine is never the constraint. On this build the machine accepts a standard encoder interface and a 1600 mm working width, which makes it straightforward to index against existing beverage filling plants conveyors.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Compare the glass bottle washing filling machine against the cost of the manual step it removes, including the labour during changeover. At 15 minutes per format change and 8446 units per hour, the saving is measurable in shift hours.

Maintenance and Service

Treat the glass bottle washing filling machine service records as production data. Once a few months of interventions are logged, the failure pattern on the seals and the drive usually tells you where the real risk sits.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the actual connected load of the glass bottle washing filling machine?

The reference build draws 23–34 kW. Confirm the starting current and the supply arrangement at the site before ordering.

Does the glass bottle washing filling machine need a specific foundation?

No special foundation is required beyond a level floor with the clearance shown on the 1600 × 2200 × 2800 mm GA drawing. Confirm the floor loading for heavy configurations.

How are changeovers recorded for traceability?

The 10-inch colour touch stores the recipe per format and the parameters can be exported. For a beverage filling plants operation running audits, request the export format in writing.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
