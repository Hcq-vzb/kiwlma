Frequently Asked Questions

Does the 20 liter jar filling machine need a specific foundation?

No special foundation is required beyond a level floor with the clearance shown on the 1800 × 3600 × 2100 mm GA drawing. Confirm the floor loading for heavy configurations.

How are changeovers recorded for traceability?

The 7-inch colour touch stores the recipe per format and the parameters can be exported. For a beverage filling plants operation running audits, request the export format in writing.

Can the 20 liter jar filling machine be upgraded to higher speed later?

Within the 16827–21327 band the drive and control have headroom. Moving beyond it means a different metering head, so plan the 8 oz range before ordering.

How do I size this for my plant?
