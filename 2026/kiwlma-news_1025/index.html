Juice Filling Machine

Published 2026-10-25 · KIWL Machine

A juice filling machine sits on the critical path of the beverage filling plants production line, which makes the selection decision more consequential than the specification sheet suggests. What follows is the working detail — capacity, contact materials, control architecture and the acceptance checks — for teams evaluating a juice filling machine.

What This Equipment Does

The practical test of a juice filling machine is not its quoted maximum but its behaviour at 17705 units per hour when product density shifts and viscosity climbs — the situation the beverage filling plants production line meets every day.

Key Technical Specifications

This juice filling machine draws 12–17 kW and occupies 2400 × 1900 × 2100 mm, uses 316L stainless steel contact parts and holds a 3.0 ml tolerance on 20 L product.

Integration With Your Line

Run the integration review with the people who operate the line, not only the people who install it. The juice filling machine is mechanically compatible with common conveyors, but the beverage filling plants workflow decides how it is actually used.

Capacity Planning and ROI

The business case for a juice filling machine is arithmetic before it is technical: 3.0 ml per unit, 17705–22205 units per hour, and a shift count that adds up across a year. Work it that way and the answer is usually within 12–36 months at regional beverage filling plants scale.

Maintenance and Service

For a beverage filling plants operation the maintenance question for a juice filling machine is simple: can the wear parts be changed by your own team during a scheduled break rather than by a technician on site?

Frequently Asked Questions

Can the juice filling machine run plastic and glass at the same time?

This configuration handles 20 L product on the specified format. Mixing materials usually means a cleaning and verification step between campaigns.

How are changeovers recorded for traceability?

The 4-inch HMI stores the recipe per format and the parameters can be exported. For a beverage filling plants operation running audits, request the export format in writing.

What accuracy can this juice filling machine hold in real production?

The reference build holds ±3.0 ml on 20 L containers at 17705–22205 bottles per hour. In a real line the number to confirm is the deviation over a full shift rather than a short test.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
