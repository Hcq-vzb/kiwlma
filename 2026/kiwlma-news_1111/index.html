Bottle Inspection System

Published 2026-11-11 · KIWL Machine

Before any bottle inspection system quotation is compared, the rate has to be tied to a product and a format. This guide does that for beverage filling plants operations and then works through the rest of the decision.

What This Equipment Does

The unit a bottle inspection system performs is easy to describe and hard to do well: it takes 330 ml product and delivers 9984–13484 units per hour, and everything downstream is calibrated against that figure.

Key Technical Specifications

Capacity without conditions is not a specification. 9984–13484 units per hour means little without 330 ml product, a 3.0 ml tolerance and a 15-minute changeover — all three stated for this bottle inspection system.

Integration With Your Line

The best commissioning plan names three people: one for the 3.0 ml verification, one for the encoder handshake, one for the cleaning route. With a 9984–13484 units per hour machine, that is what a week-long delay costs.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Model the bottle inspection system on a difficult month rather than an average one. A line running 330 ml product with a 15-minute changeover either holds up or it does not, and the investment should be judged on the harder case.

Maintenance and Service

Treat the bottle inspection system service records as production data. Once a few months of interventions are logged, the failure pattern on the seals and the drive usually tells you where the real risk sits.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can the bottle inspection system handle a 330 ml product change without full cleaning?

Changeover between compatible formats is a 15-minute operation. A material change needs the cleaning cycle, and the sequence is documented on the 4-inch HMI.

Can the bottle inspection system run without a dedicated operator?

Once the recipes are stored on the 4-inch HMI, yes. The beverage filling plants team still owns the routine checks, but the 15-minute changeover is guided.

How long does a format changeover take on this bottle inspection system?

With standardised tooling the changeover is around 15 minutes. The first change to a new 330 ml format takes longer.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
