Soda Water Production Line

Published 2026-11-17 · KIWL Machine

Three questions decide most soda water production line purchases, and they are the same for every beverage filling plants operation: what rate, what accuracy, and what happens when it stops. This guide takes them in that order.

What This Equipment Does

A soda water production line should be judged on the range it handles, not the peak it advertises. This configuration covers 500 ml can product at 8500–12000 units per hour, which is the span most beverage filling plants production lines actually demand.

Key Technical Specifications

A soda water production line is properly specified when an auditor can check it. Rated capacity 8500–12000 units per hour, contact parts 316L stainless steel, control 4-inch HMI, load 14–19 kW, footprint 1200 × 2200 × 3200 mm, changeover 12 minutes — this build carries all six.

Integration With Your Line

Confirm the working width of 1200 mm against the actual conveyor pitch, not against a catalogue. That single check removes most of the surprises that appear after a soda water production line arrives on a beverage filling plants floor.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Model the soda water production line on total cost over the equipment life, not on purchase price. A unit with 316L stainless steel contact parts and a 2.0 ml tolerance costs more to buy and less to run than a cheaper unit on the same beverage filling plants production line.

Maintenance and Service

The soda water production line was specified with wear part lead times in mind, so a beverage filling plants operation is not left waiting a week for a seal while the line stands at 8500 units per hour.

Frequently Asked Questions

How are settings recorded for traceability?

The 4-inch HMI stores a recipe per format and the parameters can be exported, which matters for beverage filling plants operations running audits.

What is the delivery lead time for the soda water production line?

Lead time depends on the control package and the format tooling. Ask for the date with the order, not separately, so the 500 ml can set is included.

Is the soda water production line suitable for continuous three-shift running?

The reference build is rated for continuous duty in the 8500–12000 band. For heavier duty ask for the upgraded drive package.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
