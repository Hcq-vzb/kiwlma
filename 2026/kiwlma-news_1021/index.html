Frequently Asked Questions

Can the 5 gallon filling line run plastic and glass at the same time?

This configuration handles 250–500 ml product on the specified format. Mixing materials usually means a cleaning and verification step between campaigns.

What accuracy can this 5 gallon filling line hold in real production?

The reference build holds ±0.3 ml on 250–500 ml containers at 15157–19657 bottles per hour. In a real line the number to confirm is the deviation over a full shift rather than a short test.

Can the 5 gallon filling line be upgraded to higher speed later?

Within the 15157–19657 band the drive and control have headroom. Moving beyond it means a different metering head, so plan the 250–500 ml range before ordering.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?
