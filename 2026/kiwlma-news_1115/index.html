Isobaric Beer Filler

Published 2026-11-15 · KIWL Machine

The isobaric beer filler is easier to specify than most beverage filling plants buyers expect, because the hard questions have already been asked by other plants. This guide sets out those questions and the answers, in the form of figures you can hold a supplier to.

What This Equipment Does

Dosing repeatability is the figure to insist on during FAT. A machine that holds ±2.0 ml on 1.5 L containers at 15752–20252 bottles per hour will keep the line within tolerance for the whole campaign, not only for the first hour.

Key Technical Specifications

The three figures that move the price most are accuracy, contact-part grade and the control platform. On this isobaric beer filler the tolerance is 2.0 ml, the contact parts are 316L stainless steel, and the controls run on a 7-inch colour touch.

Integration With Your Line

Integration usually fails at the handshake, not the mechanics. This isobaric beer filler takes a standard encoder signal and a 2400 mm working width, so it can run under the control of the line PLC rather than as an isolated island.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Payback on an isobaric beer filler should be tested against the cost of the alternative, which is people. At 15752–20252 units per hour, the manual step it removes is worth more over a year than the machine costs over five.

Maintenance and Service

Record every intervention against the isobaric beer filler. After a few months the pattern of the seals and the drive tells you the real service interval, which is usually shorter than the manual claims.

Frequently Asked Questions

What accuracy can this isobaric beer filler hold in real production?

The reference build holds ±2.0 ml on 1.5 L containers at 15752–20252 bottles per hour. In a real line the number to confirm is the deviation over a full shift rather than a short test.

Does the isobaric beer filler need a specific foundation?

No special foundation is required beyond a level floor with the clearance shown on the 2400 × 1900 × 2100 mm GA drawing. Confirm the floor loading for heavy configurations.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
