Frequently Asked Questions

What spare parts should be held for the shrink wrapping machine for bottles?

The wear items are seals, sensors and drives. For a line running 2280 units per hour, holding these on site protects availability.

Can the shrink wrapping machine for bottles handle a 8 oz product change without full cleaning?

Changeover between compatible formats is a 20-minute operation. A material change needs the cleaning cycle, and the sequence is documented on the 7-inch colour touch.

Is a 304 stainless steel contact part really necessary?

It determines the cleaning chemistry and the wear life. For corrosive or high-viscosity 8 oz product the standard grade is not appropriate.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
