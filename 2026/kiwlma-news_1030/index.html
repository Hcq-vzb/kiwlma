Automatic Can Filling Machine

Published 2026-10-30 · KIWL Machine

There is a short answer and a long answer to specifying an automatic can filling machine. The short one is the rate; the long one — and the one that determines whether the beverage filling plants production line runs — is everything the rate depends on.

What This Equipment Does

The practical test of an automatic can filling machine is not its peak rate but its behaviour at 1657 bottles per hour on a 500 ml format, where product density shifts and viscosity climbs.

Key Technical Specifications

Every quotation for an automatic can filling machine contains a headline rate and a long list of options. The four entries that actually determine the outcome are the 1.0 ml tolerance, the 316L stainless steel contact-part grade, the 32–43 kW load and the 1800 × 2800 × 3200 mm footprint.

Integration With Your Line

An automatic can filling machine fits an existing beverage filling plants production line when three things align: the transfer pitch, the control protocol and the cleaning route. This build accepts a standard encoder interface and works across a 1800 mm working width.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Capacity is only worth what you can sell. An automatic can filling machine at 1657–2307 units per hour should be sized against contracted demand for 500 ml product, otherwise the ROI depends on a market the beverage filling plants operation does not control.

Maintenance and Service

Put the automatic can filling machine on a preventive schedule that counts cycles rather than calendar months. At 1657–2307 units per hour the seals reach end of life long before a quarterly inspection notices.

Frequently Asked Questions

What accuracy can this automatic can filling machine hold in real production?

The reference build holds ±1.0 ml on 500 ml containers at 1657–2307 bottles per hour. In a real line the number to confirm is the deviation over a full shift rather than a short test.

Does the automatic can filling machine need a specific foundation?

No special foundation is required beyond a level floor with the clearance shown on the 1800 × 2800 × 3200 mm GA drawing. Confirm the floor loading for heavy configurations.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
